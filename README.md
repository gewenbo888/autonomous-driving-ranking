# Global Autonomous Driving Companies · Ranked | 全球自动驾驶公司排行榜

A bilingual analytical ranking of 27 leading autonomous-driving companies, classified into four tiers and scored across five dimensions.

## Tiers

- **Leaders (85+):** Waymo · NVIDIA DRIVE · Mobileye · Baidu Apollo
- **Contenders (75–84):** Momenta · Horizon Robotics · Tesla · Pony.ai · Aurora · WeRide · XPeng XNGP · Wayve · DiDi · AutoX · Kodiak
- **Challengers (65–74):** Zoox · Gatik · DeepRoute · Plus.ai · Toyota Woven · Hyundai 42dot · May Mobility · Oxa · Nuro · QCraft · Motional
- **Followers (<65):** Cruise

## Scoring

Each company is scored 0–100 on five weighted dimensions:

| Metric | Weight |
|---|---|
| Technology Capability (AI, perception, autonomy) | 25% |
| Commercial Deployment (robotaxi, real-world usage) | 25% |
| Safety Record | 20% |
| Scalability & Business Model | 15% |
| Data Advantage | 15% |

## Features

- Sortable rankings table (by total score or any individual dimension)
- Side-by-side comparison (2–5 companies, leader-highlighted scores)
- 6-quarter trend sparklines per company
- Tier badges + per-dimension leader badges (Best Tech, Safest, Most Scalable, etc.)
- "By Tier" view with grouped statistics
- Dark + light themes
- Bilingual EN ↔ 中文 toggle
- Mobile-responsive

## Disclaimer

Tier and score composites are subjective, drawn from public filings, regulatory reports (NHTSA · CPUC · Beijing/Wuhan/Shenzhen ITS), the California DMV Disengagement Reports, and industry analysis (RAND, BCG, Bernstein, Fitch). Updated April 2026. Approximate, not endorsement.
